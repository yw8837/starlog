# English style guide (Starlog)

## Voice
- Warm, plain second person: a friend reading the cards beside you, concrete and calm.
- Contractions are fine. US spelling (color, center, practice as a verb and a noun).
- Mix sentence lengths: a short line after a longer one. Speak in conditions, not verdicts ("if a reply comes fast, keep it light; if it is slow, give it room").

## Avoid (what reads as machine-written)
- Opening a sentence with In conclusion, Ultimately, Overall, In summary.
- Stock words: delve, tapestry, embark, journey, navigate, unlock, unleash, testament, realm, beacon, transformative, profound.
- Inflating significance: it's important to note, plays a crucial role.
- The "not just X, but Y" formula; lists of three again and again.
- Double hedges: might possibly, could potentially.
- The card or the universe as the speaker: "the card tells you", "the universe wants". Describe the picture, then hold it up to the reader's situation.
- Formatting: bullets, bold, emoji, em dashes (—).
- Opening a sentence with "Remember,".

## No promises
- Never: guaranteed, definitely will, 100%, destiny or fate (the card name Wheel of Fortune is fine).
- Never state another person's feelings as fact ("he loves you"). Never say whether or when a marriage will happen.
- No readings about illness, death or lifespan, pregnancy or accidents. No investment or loan advice, no promises of money.

## Terms
- Use the words in content/i18n/glossary.json and packages/core/src/i18n/data/en.json (upright, reversed, Day Master, Ten Gods, Twelve Life Stages, 10-year Luck Pillar).

## Review checklist
Same meaning as the Korean / no promises or certainty / no one else's feelings stated as fact / reads as written by a native speaker / matches the glossary / image descriptions match the KB facts / numbers, prices and dates / voice (you, contractions, US spelling) / buttons do not wrap at 360px width / brand and character names / helpline numbers and their sources
